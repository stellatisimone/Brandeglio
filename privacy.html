Cookie

Il sito non usa cookie. Salva soltanto nel tuo browser (nella memoria locale, localStorage) la lingua che scegli, italiano o inglese, per mostrarti la pagina nella stessa lingua alla visita successiva. Privacy and cookies

Last updated: November 2026

Who runs this website

This is an unofficial information website about Brandeglio, made by a private individual with no commercial purpose. It is not the website of the Municipality of Bagni di Lucca or of any association or body in the village.

Data collected by this website

None. The website has no forms, sign-ups, visitor statistics, advertising or social media buttons.

Cookies

The website does not use cookies. It only stores the language you choose, Italian or English, in your browser (in local storage, localStorage) so that the page opens in the same language on your next visit. This stays on your device, is not sent to anyone, and you can delete it in your browser settings.

Hosting

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like any web server, GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security and to run the service. This data is handled by GitHub under its privacy statement.

Fonts, images and maps

Fonts, photos and maps are loaded from this website itself. No requests are made to Google Fonts or other external services while you browse.

Links to other websites

Links to Google Maps, OpenStreetMap, Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons and accommodation websites open third-party sites, which apply their own privacy rules.
